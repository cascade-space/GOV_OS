## Detailed Comparison

### 1. Render (Recommended for You) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

**What it hosts:**
- ✅ Frontend (Next.js Static Site)
- ✅ Backend (Node.js Web Service)
- ✅ Database (PostgreSQL)
- ✅ All in one dashboard

**Cost:**
- Free for 90 days
- After: $14/month ($7 backend + $7 database)
- Frontend always free

**Pros:**
- ✅ Easiest setup
- ✅ Single dashboard
- ✅ Good free tier
- ✅ Automatic HTTPS
- ✅ GitHub auto-deploy
- ✅ Great documentation
- ✅ Affordable

**Cons:**
- ⚠️ Free tier sleeps after 15 min
- ⚠️ First request slow (30-60 sec)

**Best for:** Beginners, small to medium apps, testing

**Setup time:** 20 minutes

**Difficulty:** ⭐ Easy

**Setup time:** 30 minutes
